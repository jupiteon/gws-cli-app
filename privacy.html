Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

gws CLI is a personal tool used only by its owner to access their own Google accounts.

  • Google user data is accessed only on the owner's own computer, at the owner's request.
  • OAuth credentials are stored locally, encrypted, and are never transmitted to any server other than Google's.
  • No data is collected, sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and none is used for advertising.
  • The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
  • Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
